## Key learnings
- **Static-linking matters for distribution.** Dynamically linked libFLAC made the macOS binaries non-portable; static linking is what turns a build into something you can hand to someone.
- **`sandboxSafe=false` in the AU plist is the durable Logic fix** — bootstrapping it in `bootstrap-resources.sh` means it survives rebuilds instead of being re-applied by hand each time.
- **Best-effort email in registration**: a misconfigured mail transport should never 500 a signup. Auth flows shouldn't be coupled to the deliverability of a side effect.
- **libwebrtc go-live builds need an explicit toolchain contract** (C++20, `WEBRTC_MAC`) — the standalone build guide exists now because the defaults don't work.
- **Bisect instead of guessing.** When live mesh audio broke, the move was to revert the suspect commit onto a named branch (`bisect/revert-pump-ddefc9d`) and build against it, rather than patching forward blind.
- **Small protocol/lifecycle bugs are their own category** — null-channel guards in the capture writer, pong handlers on upgraded sockets, suppressing heartbeats during logout. Not glamorous, and they're what keeps a live session from falling over.
